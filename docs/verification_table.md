### Identified Source Gaps (Marked for Organizer Corpus & Human Review):
- **Adult Hypertension / Diabetes Follow-up**: The ASHA Induction Module mentions chronic illnesses generally (p.48) but does not define an adult NCD screening interval or protocol. The MoHFW Comprehensive Primary Health Care (CPHC) NCD module (30+ years, 6-monthly / yearly screening) is required for this topic.
- **MUAC Screening Cut-off**: Mid-Upper Arm Circumference (<115 mm for SAM) is covered in HBYC/IMNCI modules, but not in the Induction Module. Flagged as `NEEDS HUMAN CHECK`.
- **hbnc-hbyc.mohfw.gov.in Domain Status**: The domain `hbnc-hbyc.mohfw.gov.in` timed out during automated download attempts; primary source verification was established using the authoritative NHM India Induction Module (`nhm.gov.in`) and NHM HP STOP Diarrhoea Guidance (`nhm.hp.gov.in`).
